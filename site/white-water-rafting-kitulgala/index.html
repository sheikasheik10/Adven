White Water Rafting in Kitulgala

Kitulgala is where white water rafting in Sri Lanka began, and the Kelani River is still the best place in the country to do it. The river drops out of the central hills through thick rainforest, giving fast, splashy rapids between calm stretches where you can float, swim and look up at the jungle. Pick the 5 km run for a fun first taste or the 10 km run for the full river.

Every trip starts at our base in Kitulgala with a safety briefing, paddle practice and a full kit check: helmet, life jacket and paddle for everyone. Your raft is steered by a trained river guide who calls every stroke, so no rafting experience is needed. Between the rapids there are slow pools where the guide may let you jump in and float downstream in your life jacket.

Kitulgala sits on the A7 between Colombo and Hatton, about 2.5 hours from Colombo, so rafting here fits easily into a day trip or a stop on the way to the hill country, Nuwara Eliya or Adam's Peak. Water levels change with the monsoons, so message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you how the river is running on your date.

FAQ

White Water Rafting: your questions

Do I need to know how to swim to go rafting in Kitulgala?
Strong swimming is not required. Everyone wears a fitted life jacket and helmet, and your guide covers what to do if you fall in during the safety briefing. Please tell us if anyone in your group can't swim so the guide can plan for it.
What grade are the rapids on the Kelani River?
The Kitulgala section of the Kelani River is mostly Grade 2–3 white water, which is exciting but suitable for beginners with a guide. The 10 km run has more and bigger rapids than the 5 km run.
When is the best time for rafting in Kitulgala?
Rafting runs all year, but the river level changes with the monsoon rains and upstream hydropower releases. We check the river every morning and confirm on WhatsApp before your trip.
