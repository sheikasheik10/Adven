About this adventure

Marvel Canyoning is a step up from a beginner canyon. The route goes deeper into the rainforest, with higher cliff jumps, longer and faster natural slides, and roped descents beside falling water, all led by guides trained in rope access and rescue.

It suits fit, confident swimmers who've canyoned before or just want a bigger challenge. Expect a proper adventure day and some great photos.

  • High cliff jumps into deep pools
  • Long, fast natural slides
  • Roped waterfall sections
  • Small groups with expert guides

How the day runs

  1. BriefingHarness fitting and rope skills session.
  2. Into the canyonTrek in deep to the canyon head.
  3. Jump, slide, ropeWork down the canyon's big features.
  4. Out & refuelWalk out and celebrate at base.

What's included

  • Certified canyoning guides
  • Helmet, harness & life jacket
  • All ropes & technical gear
  • Safety briefing & rope training

What to bring

  • Swimwear or quick-dry clothes
  • Grippy shoes that can get wet
  • Towel & change of clothes
  • Water & a snack

FAQ

Good to know

Is canyoning in Kitulgala safe for beginners?
Yes. Normal Canyoning is designed for beginners. Guides check every slide and jump first, and every big jump has an easier way down. Marvel and Watagala are advanced and best after some experience.
What should I wear for canyoning?
Swimwear or quick-dry clothes, and shoes that grip on wet rock and can get wet: water shoes, trail shoes or strapped sandals. No flip-flops. Bring a towel and a change of clothes.
Can I bring my phone or camera?
Only in a waterproof case with a strap. Our guides can take photos on the route. Ask about photo packages on WhatsApp.
